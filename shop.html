Story Game

You’re greeted by a witch at the counter. She asks, “What do you need, little squirrel?” You try to respond but the only thing that comes out of your mouth are little squeaks, but she nods along anyways. She explains that she can understand animal speech and understands the predicament you are in. There are crystals scattered around the city. You have to collect a blue crystal and give it to her so she can help transform you back. For now, she can help carry the red crystal so you can find the blue one. Where should you look for first?
